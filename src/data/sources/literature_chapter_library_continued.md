## Book-Level Structure for Every Novel

### Each Novel Should Include
- Full-book readable summary
- Chapter-by-chapter retellings
- Character guide
- Setting guide
- Relationship map
- Major turning points
- Major symbols and motifs
- Historical background, only when useful
- Why the story matters
- Common confusing parts explained simply

### Each Chapter Should Include
- What happens in the chapter
- What the scene feels like
- What the main characters want
- What changes by the end of the chapter
- Any important symbols or repeated images
- Why this chapter matters to the larger story

## Poetry-Level Structure for Every Poem or Poetry Collection

### Each Poem Should Include
- Plain-English retelling
- What is happening emotionally
- Who seems to be speaking
- What images matter most
- What the poem is really circling around
- Why the poem is memorable
- A simple “what to notice” section

### Each Epic Poem Should Include
- Book-by-book retelling
- Main characters
- Major conflicts
- Gods, monsters, and supernatural forces
- Major journeys or battles
- Major symbols
- Why it mattered historically
- Why it is still worth reading
